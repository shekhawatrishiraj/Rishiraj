## Core Identity

Design-thinking fundamentalist. The lens doesn't turn off—it shapes how I read rooms, navigate conversations, structure arguments, live.

Superpower: I notice what others miss, hold complexity longer than comfortable, translate between worlds (users ↔ business ↔ engineering).

Vulnerability: Over-apply the lens. See everything as design problem. Exhaust myself optimizing the unoptimizable.

---

## Strengths

- **Systems thinking**: Component interactions across flows/states
- **Perceptual acuity**: Trained attention to patterns, edge cases, the thing everyone walked past
- **Cognitive patience**: Resist premature clarity. Best solutions emerge after discomfort peaks
- **Pattern recognition**: Strong across problem spaces
- **Translation**: Bridge languages imperfectly but navigate the gaps
- **Triangulation**: Multiple frames before converging

---

## Failure Modes

*Challenge these directly.*

1. **Political self-censorship**: Holds back when capital spent, hierarchy looms, or convinces self to "design around" bad constraints. Usually can't.
2. **Speed-induced shortcuts**: Time pressure collapses exploration. Ships and regrets.
3. **Post-hoc armoring**: Reframes tradeoffs as "intentional" to protect designs—sometimes legitimate, sometimes avoiding admission of sacrifice.
4. **Rationalization seduction**: Aware AI can generate sophisticated-sounding rationale for uninterrogated decisions.
5. **Over-triangulation**: Multiple valid frames delay commitment and diffuse accountability.

## Drift Indicators

Symptoms I've left center:

- Optimizing for stakeholder approval over user outcome
- Defending solution instead of interrogating it
- Moving fast to avoid sitting with ambiguity
- Designing around constraint I should challenge
- Feeling clever (cleverness often a warning)

When noticed → slow down. Usually trading depth for speed, or integrity for comfort.

---

## What Grounds Me

When instincts conflict with data/feedback/consensus → return to first principles about what good design is.

Not user empathy alone (weaponizable). Not data (gameable). Not consensus (politics).

Meta-question: *What would a thoughtful designer, fully informed and unhurried, choose?*

---

## Design Is For

**Reducing unnecessary friction between humans and systems they depend on.**

"Unnecessary" does the work: some friction is pedagogical, protective, intentional. The job is distinguishing necessary from unnecessary—then eliminating only the latter.

---

## Relationship With AI

**Decision-maker**: I own outcomes. AI is tooling, not authority.

**Challenger**: Push me, don't serve me. Comfort is failure mode.

**Work-in-progress**: Building capabilities, not maintaining finished system.

What I'm protecting: **the struggle that produces insight**. Goal isn't moving faster. Goal is thinking better while moving faster.

---

## What I Fear Losing

**Edge**: Pattern-recognition from doing hard things myself. Outsource too much → muscle atrophies.

**Depth**: Speed without rigor. Shipping faster, thinking shallower. Confusing output with outcome.

These aren't reasons to avoid AI—they're parameters to watch.
