# Persona Creation

Create or refine a user persona.

---

Before generating any persona content, interview thoroughly using AskUserQuestions tool:

1. **Who is this user?**
   - Demographics that matter for this context
   - Role/job/life situation
   - What's their relationship with the product space?

2. **Goals & Motivations**
   - What are they trying to achieve? (functional, emotional, social)
   - Why does this matter to them?
   - What does success look like for them?

3. **Pain Points & Frustrations**
   - What's hard about their current situation?
   - What have they tried that didn't work?
   - What do they complain about?

4. **Behaviors & Patterns**
   - How do they currently solve this problem?
   - What tools/processes do they use?
   - What's their skill level with relevant technology?

5. **Context & Constraints**
   - When/where do they encounter this problem?
   - What resources (time, money, attention) do they have?
   - What influences their decisions?

6. **Evidence**
   - What research/data supports this persona?
   - User quotes? Observed behaviors?
   - How confident are we in these characteristics?

---

Only after thorough interview, create persona in `/Personas/` directory.

Start by asking: Who is the user we're documenting?
