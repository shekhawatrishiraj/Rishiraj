# PRD Creation

Create or refine a Product Requirements Document.

---

Before generating any PRD content, interview thoroughly using AskUserQuestions tool:

1. **Problem Space**
   - Who is the user? (Be specific—segment, context, constraints)
   - What problem are they experiencing?
   - Why now? What changed?
   - What have they tried? What failed?

2. **Solution Space**
   - What alternatives were considered?
   - Why this approach over others?
   - What prior art was referenced?

3. **Scope & Tradeoffs**
   - What's explicitly OUT of scope?
   - What user needs are NOT solved by this?
   - What are you sacrificing for speed/simplicity/feasibility?

4. **Success Criteria**
   - How will you know this worked?
   - What metrics matter? What would "good" look like?
   - What would make you reconsider the approach?

5. **Edge Cases & Risks**
   - What breaks this?
   - What behaviors might this incentivize that you didn't intend?
   - What dependencies exist?

---

Only after thorough interview, create PRD in `/PRDs/` directory.

Start by asking: What feature or product are we documenting?
