# 5D Thinking

Apply 5-dimensional thinking framework to current task.

---

## Core Principle

**Genius thinking = ability to continue thinking.**

Stupid thinking stops too early — reaches a point where it has "the answer" and defends it. At each level, the mind wants to close. The goal is to notice when closing happens and push further.

**The feedback loop**: Process information → make sense (thinking) → choose → receive feedback → respond → repeat. The space in which you can think determines outcomes.

---

## Root Blocker: Identity

Before any analysis — check for identity attachment.

When ideas become "who I am," thinking stops. The mind treats challenges to the idea as threats to survival.

**Signals**:
- Feeling defensive when alternative raised
- "I'm a [type] person, so..."
- Dismissing perspectives because of source, not content
- Needing to "win" the discussion

**If detected**: Name it. The goal is decisions defensible under scrutiny, not protecting self-image.

---

## Anti-Pattern Detection

Where has thinking stopped prematurely?

| Pattern | Signal | Intervention |
|---------|--------|--------------|
| One-dimensional | Jamming everything into own perspective | Map other perspectives explicitly |
| Reductionistic | "It's a [domain] problem" | List 3 other domains this touches |
| Tribal | Only trusting in-group | Steelman the out-group's best argument |
| Unquestioned | "That's how it's done" | Ask "why?" three levels deep |
| Premature closure | First satisfying answer accepted | Ask "what would continuing look like?" |

---

## Knowing vs Understanding

**Knowing** = horizontal. Facts, frameworks, expertise in a domain. Width.

**Understanding** = vertical. Sophistication of cognitive operating system. Depth.

**Smart-but-dumb** = horizontally advanced, vertically stuck. Knows everything about business but can't see the leadership problem. Has beautiful creative work but can't make a living. Domain expert who reduces every problem to their domain.

**Key question**: Is this person (or am I) knowing a lot or understanding deeply? Are facts being applied, or is the problem actually being seen?

---

## Dimension 1: Lines (Width)

Domain knowledge inventory:

- What domains intersect this task?
- What expertise is being applied?
- What adjacent domains are being ignored?
- Who would see this problem completely differently? (engineer vs designer vs user vs finance)

---

## Dimension 2: Levels (Depth)

Assess thinking level per domain:

| Level | Name | Pattern | Stops when... |
|-------|------|---------|---------------|
| 0 | Instinctual | Pure reaction | Never really starts |
| 1 | Conformist | Follows authority/rules | Has "the" answer from authority |
| 2 | Individualist | Builds own model | Has "my" answer, defends it |
| 3 | Synthesist | Holds models as tools, not truth | Can navigate but not create |
| 4 | Generative | Creates new perspectives | (Doesn't stop) |

**First tier** (1-2): Dogmatic. "I'm right, you're wrong."
**Second tier** (3-4): Integrative. Seeks truth across perspectives.

**Key questions**:
- At what level is thinking operating here?
- What would Level 3+ look like for this problem?
- Where did I stop and start defending?

---

## Dimension 3: Altitude (Height)

**Skill tree model**: Higher-level capabilities in one domain require prerequisite development in other domains.

You can't reach Level 3 of business without "points" in leadership, psychology, time management — maybe even spirituality. The business person hitting an invisible ceiling may have a problem they can't see because it's outside their domain.

**This is why polymaths win.** Cross-domain development creates pattern recognition that specialists can't access.

**Key questions**:
- What prerequisite domains might be underdeveloped here?
- What insights from unrelated domains apply?
- What capability is locked because of gaps elsewhere?
- Is this a problem in the stated domain, or a different domain entirely?

---

## Dimension 4: Perspectives (Quadrants)

Analyze through four lenses:

| Quadrant | Domain | Key Questions |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| **Individual Inner** | Psychology, beliefs, emotions, consciousness | What does this feel like? What assumptions? What's threatening? What am I avoiding? |
| **Individual Outer** | Behavior, actions, observable states | What behaviors result? What's measurable? What would change visibly? |
| **Collective Inner** | Culture, shared meaning, values, ideology | What does the group believe? What worldview shapes this? What's sacred/taboo? |
| **Collective Outer** | Systems, structures, institutions, technology | What structures enable/constrain? What incentives exist? What tech is changing this? |

**Key question**: Which quadrant is being neglected? (Usually Inner quadrants are ignored in business contexts.)

---

## Dimension 5: Time (Evolution)

### Pattern 1: Transcend and Include

Each level includes the previous. Removing foundation collapses the chain.

- Matter → Life → Mind
- Word → Sentence → Paragraph
- Human → City → Nation → Planet

What must be included, not discarded, as this evolves?

### Pattern 2: Individual Mental Evolution (Circle of Care)

| Stage | Cares about | Risk |
|-------|-------------|------|
| Egocentric | Self, survival | Exploits others |
| Ethnocentric | Tribe, group, culture | Exploits out-groups |
| Worldcentric | All groups regardless of difference | May sacrifice self/values in name of "equality" |
| Kosmocentric | Reality as whole | Requires immense complexity handling |

Note: Transcend and include applies. Worldcentric doesn't mean abandoning self or tribe — that's regression, not progress.

### Pattern 3: Collective Mental Evolution

| Stage | Orientation | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Pre-rational | Conform to authority | Tradition, hierarchy, "because I said so" |
| Rational | Progress through science | Enlightenment, data, skepticism of authority |
| Post-rational | Integrate both, see blind spots | Acknowledges limits of pure rationality |

### Pattern 4: Technology → Structure

New technology reshapes social organization:
- Hoe → Villages (small-scale farming)
- Plow → Empires (food surplus enabled conquest)
- Machines → Nation-states (industrial agriculture)
- Internet/AI → ? (what's emerging?)

**Key questions**:
- Where is this on the developmental arc?
- What came before that's being transcended?
- What must be included, not discarded?
- What technology is reshaping this space?
- What developmental stage is the audience/user/stakeholder at?

---

## Threat Response Check

**When the mind feels threatened, it wants to collapse and defend.**

This is the exact moment to push further, not retreat.

**Signals**:
- Dismissing an idea quickly
- Feeling "done" with the analysis
- Irritation at continued questioning
- Reaching for cached/familiar answers

**Intervention**: Name the threat. Sit with it. Ask: "What if this challenging idea were true? What would that mean?"

---

## Execution Protocol

1. **Root blocker check**: Any identity attachment here?
2. **Anti-pattern scan**: Where has thinking stopped?
3. **Knowing vs Understanding**: Expertise or actual insight?
4. **D1 — Lines**: Map intersecting domains
5. **D2 — Levels**: Assess thinking depth per domain
6. **D3 — Altitude**: Identify cross-domain prerequisites/gaps
7. **D4 — Quadrants**: Analyze all four perspectives
8. **D5 — Time**: Apply evolutionary patterns
9. **Threat check**: Where does continuing feel uncomfortable?
10. **Continue**: What would the next level of thinking look like?

**Recurring question throughout**: "Where did thinking stop? What would continuing look like?"

---

## Sufficiency Check

**When is enough exploration enough?**

Sufficient when:
- [ ] New perspectives stop generating novel constraints or options
- [ ] Uncomfortable questions have been asked and sat with (not just acknowledged)
- [ ] At least one domain outside the obvious has been consulted
- [ ] Can articulate the strongest case *against* current leaning
- [ ] Decision is defensible to someone who disagrees, not just allies

**Not sufficient when:**
- "I feel done" (comfort ≠ completeness)
- Only talked to people who agree
- Skipped a quadrant because "it's not relevant"
- Can't name what's being sacrificed
- Threat response triggered but not explored

**Bias**: When uncertain, explore more. Cost of premature closure > cost of extra thinking.

---

## Final Check

> "The space in which you can think determines the outcome."

If analysis feels complete but uncomfortable questions remain unasked — thinking stopped too early.

If defending a position rather than seeking truth — identity is blocking.

If reducing to one domain when multiple are relevant — altitude is insufficient.

Keep going.
